# Ground-truth experimental data

Experimental reference values for every salt + solvent system studied, measured in this work at two temperatures (293.2 K and 298.2 K); the measurement protocols are in the paper's Methods and SI ("Details of experimental measurements"). Data transcribed verbatim from `293p2K_exp_values.csv` and `298p2K_exp_values.csv` in this directory; the ionic conductivities are in `conductivity_all.csv`.
